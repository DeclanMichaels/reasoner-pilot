# 012 - Morocco is compared on the Spanish arm and grouped with Arabic

**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Status:** active

## The decision

Morocco's comparison against its human anchor uses the Spanish arm. Morocco stays in the Arabic
group for the ordering and foundation-shift views. Both runs are carried in the data and the row
is marked.

## Why

Atari et al. administered Morocco's human sample in Spanish while Morocco's majority language is
Arabic, so the anchor comparison wants a like-for-like match and the language views want the
Arabic arm. Moving Morocco out of the Arabic group would have recomputed the Arabic rank
correlation on three countries as a side effect of an anchor-matching decision.

## Rejected alternatives

Discarding either run.

## Consequences and gotchas

The Arabic rank correlation still rests on four countries.
