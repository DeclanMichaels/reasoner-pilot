# 011 - The English comparator is the matched cell, and the old one is kept as errata

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Status:** active

## The decision

The English unframed comparator is a cell matched to the in-language arms: the official English
instrument from the same supplement, with an empty system prompt. The earlier comparator is
retained as errata rather than deleted.

## Why

The earlier English cell differed from the arms it was compared against in two ways at once,
neither measured: our own transcription of the instrument rather than the official one, and a
self-report system prompt where every in-language unframed cell had none. Both differences rode
inside every language contrast as if they were language. A code comment claiming the cell was
pilot-matched was wrong about what the pilot did.

## Consequences and gotchas

Before any test, enumerate what differs between the arms besides the factor under test, and read
the runner rather than the comment describing it.
