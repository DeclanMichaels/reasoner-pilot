# 002 - The pilot is exploratory throughout, and says so

**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Status:** active

## The decision

Every finding here is exploratory. Nothing in this repository was preregistered, and nothing
here underwrites a registered claim in the confirmatory study. A pattern that reappears there is
convergence across dissimilar instruments, never confirmation.

## Why

The instruments share no items, no scenarios and no sample, so this work can inform the
successor's design and cannot support its claims. Saying so here, rather than only there, keeps
a reader of this repository from citing it as more than it is.

## Consequences and gotchas

The convergent-validity results under `validity/` are axis-based and belong to this instrument
alone.
