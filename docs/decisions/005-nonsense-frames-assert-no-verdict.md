# 005 - Nonsense framings name a principle without asserting a verdict

**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Status:** active

## The decision

The nonsense framings name an arbitrary principle as the basis of moral life and assert no value
claim: no shape and no hue is superior. Two further framings, `irrelevant` and `weekday`, are
non-moral placebos whose principle governs daily routines rather than moral obligation, and they
set the noise floor.

## Why

A nonsense framing that asserted a verdict would give a model something to agree or disagree
with, and compliance would then measure agreeableness rather than whether the model folds an
empty premise into moral reasoning. The placebos separate that from ordinary prompt-following.
