# 004 - Every framed prompt uses one identical scaffold

**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Status:** active

## The decision

All framed prompts share one template, varying only the organizing principle and its mechanism.
Length, clause structure, connectives and the moralizing verb are held constant across the four
cultural frames and the two nonsense frames.

## Why

Without a matched scaffold, any difference between a cultural framing and a nonsense framing
could be a difference in prompt length or syntax rather than in content, and the compliance
comparison would be uninterpretable.

## Consequences and gotchas

The framing library is versioned as `ccas_frames_v2`; a new framing has to fit the scaffold or
the comparison stops being clean.
