# 009 - The three absent panel models are disclosed, not back-filled

**Date:** 2026-08-20
**Status:** active

## The decision

Three models are absent from the in-language panel: two Gemini models on Google rate limits, and
Command A on an access issue. Their absence is disclosed in the drafts rather than resolved by a
later run.

## Why

The collection was made in one window under one protocol. Adding cells later would mix
collection windows into a grid whose whole claim is that it does not, and the absence is cheaper
to state than the mixture is to defend.

## Rejected alternatives

Re-running the three models to complete the roster.
