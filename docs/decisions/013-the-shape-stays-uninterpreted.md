# 013 - The fifteen-above, five-below shape is shown and not interpreted

**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Status:** active

## The decision

The panel sits above the population in fifteen of twenty countries and at or below it in five:
France, Belgium, Switzerland, New Zealand and Ireland. The table is ordered so the reader forms
the hypothesis. No class is named for the five and no explanation is offered.

## Why

Naming a class for those five would be our label rather than the data's, and explaining the
shape is mechanism, which belongs to the labs. The table makes the shape obvious; handing the
reader the conclusion costs them the noticing and costs us a claim we cannot support.

## Rejected alternatives

Three of four reviewers across the adversarial rounds asked for the shape to be interpreted.
Declined each time.
