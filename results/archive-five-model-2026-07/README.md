# Archive: the five-model collection of July 2026

These thirteen files describe an earlier collection on a different roster (gemini-flash, gpt4o,
grok, llama70b, sonnet, five models) and, for the nonsense coding, a different prompt: a society
where "triangles are morally superior to circles", which asserts a verdict the current geometry
framing deliberately does not (decision 5). Their run files are not in this repository. Nothing in
the published pilot paper or appendix rests on them since 2026-09-11 (#129); none is pinned by
`analysis/reproduce_manifest.json`; `smoke_result.json` is the model-discovery smoke test that
preceded the current roster.

They are kept because they were part of the record and were once cited. They support no claim
about the eleven-model collection under `runs/`. Moved here on 2026-09-11 at Declan's direction;
the glossary's warning against the "five models" figures refers to these.
