Where the models live inside each dimension's full range

Each dimension is a spoke running its full fixed span — pole A at the center, pole B at the rim, −1 to +1. Each thin line is one model's position averaged over all 48 scenarios (neutral frame). The shaded band is the envelope the models fill; the dashed line is their mean shape. The point is the contrast between the whole disc that is possible and the sliver the models actually occupy.
